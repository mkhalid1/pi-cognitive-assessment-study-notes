## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not PI's: the practice formats and items, the methods and traps lists, the pacing plan and the 20-second rule, the study plans, the diagnostic thresholds, and the suggestion to aim for 35 to 40 items attempted with few slips. PI does not publish question types beyond the three content areas, so the formats in the section guides are practice formats, not a description of the real item pool.
