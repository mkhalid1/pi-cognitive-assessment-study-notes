# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when the invitation is already in your inbox. Both start with the [15-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know your weakest area and your pace. The 7-day plan takes about 30 to 45 minutes a day.

PI says the test needs no preparation, and it is true that you cannot learn the answers in advance. What practice does change is familiarity and speed. A first-time candidate loses seconds on every new question style. A prepared one does not.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read [the test explained](test-overview.md) and [How scoring works](scoring.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Note your weakest area and how many items you reached in time. |
| 2 | Weakest area | Read that area's guide. Do its 15 practice items untimed, then again in 3 minutes 30 seconds. Review every miss. |
| 3 | Numerical | Read the [numerical guide](sections/numerical-reasoning.md). Learn the series checklist. Do the items timed. Spend 10 minutes on mental arithmetic: quarters, tenths, times tables. |
| 4 | Verbal and abstract | Do the [verbal](sections/verbal-reasoning.md) and [abstract](sections/abstract-reasoning.md) sets, timed. For each abstract miss, write the rule in five words or fewer. |
| 5 | First full timed run | Read the [timing guide](timing.md). Take a full 50-question, 12-minute practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pi-cognitive-assessment?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pi-cognitive-assessment). Note how many you answered and how many were right. |
| 6 | Fix the leak | If you answered too few, drill the quick types (series, analogies, simple shapes) for speed. If you made too many slips, redo your missed items slowly and find the trap you fell for. |
| 7 | Light run and rest | If your invitation includes PI's official sample questions, do them now. Then read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

For each miss, answer three questions:

1. **Was it speed or understanding?** Did you run out of time, guess, or genuinely get it wrong?
2. **What should have tipped me off?** A "not" in the question, a second rule in a shape series, the starting value in a percentage change.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "read the question line first".

Keep these in one list. After a few days you will see the same two or three slips repeating. Fixing those is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when the link has arrived and the deadline is close.

### Day 1: learn the formats

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 15 min | Read "How the questions look" and "Traps" in all three section guides. |
| 3 | 30 min | Do the practice set for your weakest area, then one other set, both timed. Read every explanation. |
| 4 | 15 min | Take a full timed run, such as the [free PI practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pi-cognitive-assessment?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pi-cognitive-assessment). |
| 5 | 10 min | Review your misses with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: sit the test

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min | Redo yesterday's missed items without the answers. |
| 2 | 5 min | Read the [timing guide](timing.md) and the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 3 | 5 min | If your invitation has PI's sample questions, do them as a warm-up. |
| 4 | 12 min | Sit the real test in a quiet room, at a time of day when you are usually sharp. |

If you only have one evening, do day 1 blocks 1, 2 and 4, then sit the test the next morning.

## What not to do

- **Do not hunt for "real" questions.** PI builds each test from a large pool using its LOFT engine, so leaked questions will not match yours. Learn the formats instead.
- **Do not cram the night before.** On a 12-minute speed test, being rested and alert is worth more than one more practice set.
- **Do not judge yourself by a practice score.** PI says practice results do not predict the real score. Use them to find what to fix.
- **Do not try to get help during the test.** PI says it blurs the screen and blocks copy and paste if you navigate away, and it is against the employer's rules. It would also mean a job matched to someone else's ability.
